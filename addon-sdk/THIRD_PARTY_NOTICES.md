# Third-Party Notices

This package bundles `jose` (JWT signature verification) as its one runtime dependency. The
generated manifest validators also include Unicode-length logic derived from AJV and
deep-equality logic derived from fast-deep-equal — those two are not runtime dependencies, only
ported logic. All three projects are distributed under the MIT License; their notices follow.
